Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use the TaxCalc Pro - Aaykar Ganana application ("the App").

1. Data Collection and File Processing

The App provides a feature to upload your original GA55 document to extract and read data for tax calculations. Please note that all document processing, data extraction, and PDF generation happen locally on your device. We do not upload, store, or share your GA55 documents, personal information, or financial data on any external servers.

2. Live Updates & Firebase Services

The App uses Firebase Realtime Database (by Google) to fetch live breaking news banners, tax circulars, and notifications. This service only retrieves public information published by the admin and does not collect or track your personal financial data.

3. Advertisements and Third-Party Services

The App is supported by advertisements. We use third-party advertising services (such as Google AdMob) to serve Banner, Interstitial, and App Open ads. These third-party networks may collect and use non-personal data, such as your device's Advertising ID, IP address, and usage information, to provide personalized or non-personalized ads. We do not share your tax data or GA55 information with these advertisers.

4. Internet Permission

The App requires internet permission to fetch live tax updates, load advertisements, check for app updates, and open official PDF links. It does not use the internet to secretly upload your private tax data.

5. Local Storage

The App saves your preferences, salary entries, and generated PDF files locally on your device's storage using SharedPreferences for your convenience. This data remains strictly on your device.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately upon posting.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at: pc.muhal@gmail.com

1. डेटा संग्रह और फ़ाइल प्रोसेसिंग

यह ऐप आपको अपना मूल GA55 प्रपत्र अपलोड करके डेटा पढ़ने की सुविधा देता है। कृपया ध्यान दें कि दस्तावेज़ पढ़ने, डेटा निकालने और PDF बनाने की पूरी प्रक्रिया केवल आपके मोबाइल/डिवाइस के अंदर ही होती है। हम आपके GA55 दस्तावेज़ या किसी भी व्यक्तिगत/वित्तीय डेटा को अपने सर्वर पर अपलोड, स्टोर या शेयर नहीं करते हैं।

2. लाइव अपडेट और फ़ायरबेस सेवाएं

ऐप लाइव ब्रेकिंग न्यूज़ बैनर, टैक्स सर्कुलर और नोटिफिकेशन प्राप्त करने के लिए Firebase Realtime Database का उपयोग करता है। यह सेवा केवल एडमिन द्वारा पब्लिश की गई सार्वजनिक जानकारी को डाउनलोड करती है और आपके निजी वित्तीय डेटा को ट्रैक नहीं करती है।
