Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use the Tax Calculator - Aaykar Ganana application ("the App").

1. Data Collection and File Processing

The App provides a feature to upload your original GA55 document to extract and read data for tax calculations. Please note that all document processing, data extraction, and PDF generation happen locally on your device. We do not upload, store, or share your GA55 documents, personal information, or financial data on any external servers.

2. Advertisements and Third-Party Services

The App is supported by advertisements. We use third-party advertising services (such as Google AdMob) to serve these ads. These third-party networks may collect and use non-personal data, such as your device's Advertising ID, IP address, and usage information, to provide personalized or non-personalized ads. We do not share your tax data or GA55 information with these advertisers.

3. Internet Permission

The App requires internet permission primarily to fetch and display advertisements, check for necessary updates, or open external informational links. It does not use the internet to secretly upload your private data.

4. Local Storage

The App saves your preferences and generated PDF files locally on your device's storage for your convenience. This data remains strictly on your device.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately upon posting.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at: pc.muhal@gmail.com
