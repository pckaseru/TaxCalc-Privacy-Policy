Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use the Tax Calc. 2026-27 - Aaykar Ganana application ("the App").

1. Data Collection and File Processing

The App provides a feature to upload your original GA55 document to extract and read data for tax calculations. Please note that all document processing, data extraction, and PDF generation happen locally on your device. We do not upload, store, or share your GA55 documents, personal information, or financial data on any external servers.

2. App Lock & Biometric Security (New)

To enhance your privacy, the App offers an "App Lock" feature utilizing your device's built-in fingerprint or PIN authentication. We do not collect, store, transmit, or have direct access to your biometric data (fingerprints or face scans). This authentication is processed entirely by your Android device's native operating system to securely unlock the App locally.

3. Live Updates & Firebase Services

The App uses Firebase Realtime Database (by Google) to fetch live breaking news banners, tax circulars, and notifications. This service retrieves public information published by the admin.

4. User Comments & Anonymous Device ID (UUID)

The App features a discussion/reply section for news updates. If you choose to post a comment, the name you provide (optional) and your message are stored securely in our Firebase database. To allow you to manage and edit your comments without requiring a user login/account, the App generates an anonymous, random Unique Identifier (UUID) saved locally on your device. We do not access, collect, or track your real hardware Device ID (such as IMEI or MAC address). The UUID is used strictly for managing your comments within the app.

5. Advertisements and Third-Party Services

The App is supported by advertisements. We use third-party advertising services (such as Google AdMob) to serve Banner, Interstitial, and App Open ads. These third-party networks may collect and use non-personal data, such as your device's Advertising ID, IP address, and usage information, to provide personalized or non-personalized ads. We do not share your tax data or GA55 information with these advertisers.

6. Internet Permission

The App requires internet permission to fetch live tax updates, load advertisements, check for app updates, post user comments, and open official PDF links. It does not use the internet to secretly upload your private tax data.

7. Local Storage

The App saves your preferences, salary entries, anonymous UUID, App Lock status, and generated PDF files locally on your device's storage for your convenience. This data remains strictly on your device.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately upon posting.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at: pc.muhal@gmail.com
